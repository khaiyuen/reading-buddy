# The Secret Explorers — verified research notes

Researched 2026-10-29 for rebuilding `content/secret-explorers.ts`.
Series: **The Secret Explorers** by **SJ King**, illustrated by Ellie O'Shea, published by **DK**.

Confidence tags used below:
- **[PUB]** — publisher text (DK / Penguin Random House) or a publisher-fed retailer/library record.
- **[BOOK]** — taken from the book's own printed matter: Google Books table of contents and
  "common terms and phrases" index for that exact edition.
- **[REV]** — a single reader review. Weak.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** — not confirmed. Do not write quiz content from this.

---

## 1. Series ground truth

**14 books**, published 2020–2023. Numbering 1–13 is agreed by DK/PRH and Goodreads; *Cave Crisis*
is numbered 14 only by library catalogs (PRH and Goodreads omit it from the series list). No book 15
found; nothing says the series has ended. **[PUB]**

- Ages **7–9**, grades 2–4. Book 1 is Lexile **600L**. **[PUB]**
  (The app currently says ages 5–8 — too low.)
- **128 pages** per book, **8 chapters** per book, plus back matter. **[PUB / BOOK]**
- Back matter in every book: "*<Explorer>*'s Mission Notes" (the science recap), a quiz, a glossary. **[PUB]**

### How the premise actually works
- A **glowing compass badge** on the explorer's shirt lights up, and a matching compass sign appears
  on a nearby door. They step through that door into the **Exploration Station**. **[PUB / REV]**
- The Exploration Station picks **exactly two** explorers per mission, by the skills needed. The other
  six appear as cameos and sometimes help from base. **[PUB / REV]**
- They travel in the **Beagle** (named after Darwin's ship): a battered old go-kart that shape-shifts
  into whatever the mission needs — submarine, spacecraft, snowmobile, motor glider, desert buggy.
  It communicates in beeps. **[BOOK — "Beagle", "go-kart" appear in every book's term index]**
- **There is no character or device called "the Beeper".** The current app content invented it.
  The beeping thing is the Beagle. **[UNVERIFIED that any "Beeper" exists; no source mentions one]**
- Who runs the Station: **[UNVERIFIED]**. Reviewers note there are no adults helping.

### The eight explorers
Expertise labels are DK's own wording. **[PUB]**

| Name | Expertise |
|---|---|
| Connor | Marine life / oceans |

Home countries are **[UNVERIFIED]** except Kiki (Ghana) and Gustavo (Brazil), each from a single
reader review. Correct spelling is **Gustavo**, not "Gustava".

## 3. What the app currently has, and how wrong it is

`content/secret-explorers.ts` has 10 books. **6 of the 10 titles do not exist.** The 4 real titles
are all numbered wrongly. All 50 questions are generic and were written without a source — they use
hedges like "like pollution or noise" and "a natural disaster — like a volcanic eruption or
flooding", and they invent a device called "the Beeper".

## 4. The verification ceiling (read before rebuilding)

Online sources give, reliably: **titles, order, dates, which two explorers go, setting, the problem
as DK states it, the real 8-chapter titles (7 of 8 for most books), and the book's own index of
names and science terms.**

They do **not** give **endings or scene-by-scene events** for any of the 14 books. No summary site
(SparkNotes, SuperSummary, etc.) covers this series; reader reviews are two or three lines.

So, under CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md rule 1 and rule 3, questions about *how a book ends* or *what
happens in chapter N* cannot be written from these sources without inventing — which is the exact
mistake that produced the current content.

Two honest ways forward:
1. **Setup-level questions only.** Ask about what is verified: who goes, where, why, what the
   problem is, which animal or science idea is at the centre. Leave `verified: false` on anything
   beyond that, and skip "how did it end" questions.
2. **Read the books.** Six titles have live Google Books previews (Lost Whales, Comet Collision,
   Rainforest Rangers, Smoking Volcano, Ice Age Adventure, Haunted Castle, Desert Disappearance,
   Sunken Treasure), which render as page images that can be read a page at a time — slow but
   genuine. Otherwise the physical copies are the ground truth.

## 5. Verification log

Checks run on 2026-10-29 after writing these notes:

- **DK's own catalogue** (dk.com, the publisher) lists the real titles and confirms
  *Comet Collision* as "this second adventure", matching the numbering above.
- **The six invented titles are absent from DK's catalogue.** Searching dk.com for
  "secret explorers ancient library" and "secret explorers missing mammals" returns only the real
  books (Tomb Robbers, Jurassic Rescue, Cave Crisis, Haunted Castle, Lost Whales, Missing Scientist,
  Desert Disappearance). No such books exist.
- **The Kiki/Leah conflict in book 5 is settled** by DK's product page, which names Kiki. Corrected
  above from an inference to publisher-verified.
- **The 44% distractor figure was measured**, not estimated: 22 of 50 questions in the current
  `content/distractors/secret-explorers.ts` have the correct answer as the longest option.
- These notes are documentation only. No `.ts` content file was modified; the app typechecks
  (`tsc --noEmit` clean) and `npm run build` succeeds. The 6 `npm run lint` errors are pre-existing,
  in `ChapterPickerClient.tsx`, `QuizClient.tsx` and `useProgress.ts`, none of them touched here.
